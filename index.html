Lily Pad Math

A talking math game for kids ages 3 to 7. Pip the frog reads every question aloud, and the game adjusts as your child plays, following kindergarten and 1st grade math.

For iPhone and iPad

  • No reading neededEvery question is spoken, so pre-readers can play on their own.
  • Grows with your childA little harder when they're cruising, a little easier when they need it.
  • Private by designNo ads, no accounts, no tracking. Progress stays on the device.
