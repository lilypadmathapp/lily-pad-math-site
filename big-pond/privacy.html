Lily Pad Math: Big Pond Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

The short version: Lily Pad Math: Big Pond does not collect, share or sell any information about you or your child. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What the game stores, and where

To work, the game remembers a few things on the device it is played on:

  • The names (or nicknames) and school grade a grown-up enters for each player.
  • Each player's game progress: skill levels, answers right on the first try, pond friends and badges.
  • Settings, such as whether questions are read aloud and which voice is used.

This information is saved only in the app's own storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive it and no way to see it.

If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, Apple's backup may include the app's saved progress, as it does for most apps. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, email addresses or passwords.
  • No advertising. There is one optional purchase, the full version, and only a grown-up can reach it, behind a grown-ups-only check.
  • No analytics, tracking or third-party software that collects data.
  • No use of the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • The iPad and iPhone app works fully offline. The only time it goes online is to check with Apple's App Store whether the full version was bought, or when a grown-up buys or restores it.
  • No links to other websites or apps for children to follow.

Buying the full version

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, using the grown-up's Apple ID. We never see names, email addresses, payment details or Apple IDs. The app only learns whether the full version has been bought on this Apple ID, so it can unlock the games.

Spoken questions

The game reads questions aloud using the text-to-speech voices built into your device. The speech is created on your device. Nothing your child does is recorded, and the game never listens.

Children's privacy

Big Pond is made for children in 2nd and 3rd grade. Because it does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children, including under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws in other countries. Settings are kept behind a grown-ups-only check so children can't change them by accident.

Deleting information

A grown-up can remove a player or erase a player's progress at any time in the Grown-ups corner (press and hold the Grown-ups button, then answer the grown-ups question). Deleting the app from your device erases everything it stored.

Our website

The web page where this policy and our support page are posted is delivered by a hosting service. Like most web hosts, that service may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running. We don't receive or use those logs. The app never sends anything to the website.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. We don't plan to ever start collecting information about children.
