Lily Pad Math Support

Lily Pad Math is a talking math game for kids ages 3 to 7. Pip the frog asks questions out loud, and the game adjusts to each child as they play, following kindergarten and 1st-grade math.

Get help

Questions, problems or ideas? Email lilypadmathapp@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

How do I open the grown-ups settings?
Press and hold the Grown-ups button at the top right for about a second, then type the number shown in words. There you can add or remove players, change ages and levels, pick a voice, and turn reading aloud on or off.

The voice sounds robotic.
On iPad or iPhone, download a better voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English (for example Ava or Zoe, Premium). Then open the Grown-ups corner in the app and pick it from the Voice menu.

There's no sound.
Check that the device volume is up, and that "Read questions aloud" is on in the Grown-ups corner.

The game is too easy or too hard.
It adjusts on its own: four right answers out of five moves a skill up, and a run of misses moves it down. You can also raise or lower any skill with the + and − buttons in the Grown-ups corner. For a kindergartner who already counts to 20, use "Give a head start".

What's free, and what does the full version add?
The free version includes 5 ponds of play, one child, and one mission with each bonus team. The full version is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited play, every bonus mission, and as many children as you like. No subscription and no ads. Family Sharing is included.

I bought the full version. How do I get it on another device?
Open the Grown-ups corner and choose "Restore purchase", signed in with the same Apple ID (or a Family Sharing member).

How do I start over?
In the Grown-ups corner, choose the player's tab, then "Start over" (erases that player's progress) or "Remove player".

Will progress move to a new device?
Progress is saved only on the device. If you restore a new device from an iCloud or computer backup, the app's progress comes with it.
